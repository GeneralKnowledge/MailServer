First: set up your mail apps

  1. Open your mailbox settings and create an app password under App passwords, one per app or device.
  2. In the mail app, sign in with your address and that app password. Your account password only works in the browser.
  3. On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, the same settings page offers a setup profile that also adds calendars and contacts.

Security

  • Password and two-factor sign-in: change your password, add an authenticator app or a security key, and make backup codes for when you lose your phone
  • Where you're signed in (sign out other devices)
  • Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page: the link goes to this mailbox, which your mail app can still read. Otherwise ask an administrator.
  • App passwords and recent mail-app logins

Mail

  • Webmail: mail, contacts, calendar, filters and vacation replies

Privacy and spam

  • Spam filter: in your mailbox settings, the Spam filter tab sets how strict filtering is, and keeps lists of senders to always allow or always block
  • Quarantine: messages held as suspicious, and how often you hear about them (Settings in your mailbox settings)
  • Images from other websites are not loaded in webmail, so senders can't see when you open a message. To change it: Webmail → Preferences → Mail → Display remote inline images.
